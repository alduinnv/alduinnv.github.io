Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how A26 Content Creation ("A26"), a personal tool operated by Aldwin Nou, handles data from connected TikTok accounts.

Data we access

  • TikTok basic profile info: open ID, display name, username, avatar (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload and post videos the account owner chooses (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • Upload status returned by TikTok (publish ID, post ID).

How data is used

Data is used only to show which account is connected and to upload and post the owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold or shared with anyone.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the operator's own computer. Tokens are stored locally in a private folder and are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in A26 deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access in TikTok settings at any time.
