A26 Content Creation

A26 is a personal content production tool used by a single creator to prepare short videos and publish them to the creator's own social media accounts, including TikTok.

What it does with TikTok

  • The creator logs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect their own account.
  • The creator uploads a finished video they produced, with its caption, to their own TikTok profile through the Content Posting API.
  • The app reads basic profile info (display name, username, avatar) only to show which account a video will be posted to.

A26 is not a public service. It runs on the creator's own computer and is not offered to other users.
